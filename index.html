Sources. State results and electoral votes: Wikipedia's per-election results tables (drawn from official state canvasses and the National Archives), cross-checked against the MIT Election Data + Science Lab state returns, 1976–2024. County results: MIT Election Data + Science Lab (2000–2016) and T. McGovern's county-level results dataset (2020–2024). Where county totals don't add up to the official state total (overseas ballots, write-ins), the difference is kept as a statewide remainder. Alaska reports results by legislative district, not county, so it has no county map. Map shapes: U.S. Census Bureau via us-atlas.
